AmbiSense

Describe the atmosphere you want. Results are ranked by what reviewers actually said, with the sentences for and against each place.

    How ranking works
    1. The query is split into atmosphere targets (noise, energy, space, romance, lighting, music), using a fixed lexicon. Negations like "not too loud" flip the target to its opposite ("quiet").
    2. For each target, review sentences about that aspect are retrieved with BM25 and dense embeddings, fused with reciprocal rank fusion.
    3. An NLI model scores each sentence from −1 (contradicts the target) to +1 (supports it). A restaurant's score for the target is the similarity-weighted average over its top 10 sentences, pulled toward the overall average when it has little evidence.
    4. The final score is the mean over targets, from −1 to +1.
